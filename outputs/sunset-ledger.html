What the Los Angeles reverse directories list at each street number, 1906–1987, with each lot classified by land use. Transcribed from the LAPL pages; names kept as printed.

Lots by land use

One lot = a street number plus its ½ ¼ ¾ addresses. Only lots with at least one listing that year are counted.

How lots are classified

  • Each listing is typed first: business, institutional, household, named apartment building, or vacant. The lot (street number plus any ½ ¼ ¾ addresses) then gets one land use per year.
  • Mixed-use: households and businesses or institutions on the same lot, including a store at 1531 with flats at 1531¼–¾.
  • Multifamily: a named apartment building or APARTMENT heading, any listing by unit number, households at two or more addresses on the lot (1452 and 1452½), or three or more households at one number.
  • Single-family: one household at the number. Two different surnames at one number with nothing else is also filed single-family, with a note, since a directory can't tell boarders from a second unit.
  • Institutional: post office, DWP office, veterans’ post, lodge, church office, nonprofits, unions and professional associations. A lot with both businesses and institutions is filed commercial.
  • A lot only exists in a year if something is listed there. A ½ address that drops out of a year can flip a lot between multifamily and single-family; that reflects the listing, not necessarily the building.

Confidence

listing
high: a trade word, business name, unit number, or 1987 mixed case for a resident. medium: a bare personal name in a year whose directory normally gives trades (1906–60, 1973), or a business identified from the same name or phone at the same address in another year. low: a bare name in the 1964–68 printouts with no other evidence, a 1987 personal name in capitals, a name with no trade (“El Rodeo”), or a guess from outside knowledge I haven’t verified.
lot
High if the category holds using only high-confidence listings; medium if it needs medium ones; low if it rests on a low-confidence listing.

A bare personal name at a storefront address may be the proprietor rather than a household. Where a business shares the exact address, the household call is downgraded one step.

Sources

Every year covers the full 1400–2100 stretch. 1964, 1965 and 1968 split Sunset between Section 1 (up to the 1630s) and Section 11 (1700 on); both halves are included. 1973 and 1987 split the stretch across ZIP codes 90012 and 90026; listings are merged by address.

Transcription notes

  • 1956 p.721, 1964 p.41, 1965 p.40 and both 1906 and 1927 pages are images with no text layer; I read them from the scans. The others have a text layer, which I checked against the page image.
  • 1968 prints EICKHOFF W at 1446, inside the run of 1540s addresses; likely 1546. 1968 prints SUNSET CAMERA at 1643; every other year has it at 1634 with the same phone. Both filed as printed.
  • On the north side, 1906 puts Alvarado between 1945 and 2013, while 1956 has 2035 and 2105 both at Sunset & Alvarado. The 1906 listings at 2013 and 2015 may not be the same lots as later years. The south side and 1400–1900 line up across years.
  • 1987 sets businesses in bold capitals; 1964–68 are all capitals; other years are mixed case. A trailing “r” in 1956–60 is recorded but not interpreted.
