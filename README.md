# Sunset Blvd 1400–2100: reverse-directory dataset, 1906–1987

What the Los Angeles reverse (street-address) directories list at every street number on Sunset Blvd from 1400 to 2100 (Echo Park), for nine years: 1906, 1927, 1956, 1960, 1964, 1965, 1968, 1973, 1987. Each listing is typed (business, household, apartment building, institutional, vacant), each business is described and bucketed, and each lot gets a land use per year.

Built for a USC Price group project (planning theory for social justice) that uses the strip's changing business mix to tell the story of the neighborhood.

**Start with `HANDOFF.md` if you are picking up the business-research task.**

## Layout

```
data/
  transcriptions/YEAR.txt      hand transcription of each year's pages (the source of truth)
  sources.json                 directory title and pages for each year
  derived/                     everything below is regenerated by the scripts
    listings_flagged.csv       one row per printed listing + formatting flags
    listings_classified.csv    + use type, business description, bucket, confidence, reasoning, lot land use
    listings.csv               + stable listing_id and entity_id  ← use this one
    by_address_wide.csv        one row per printed address, one column per year
    landuse_by_lot.csv         one row per lot, land use / confidence / buckets / notes per year
    summary.json               counts by year (land use; lots with a business per bucket)
research/
  research_queue.csv           one row per business entity to look up, in priority order
  findings.csv                 empty template for research results (see HANDOFF.md)
scripts/
  build.py                     transcriptions → listings_flagged.csv, by_address_wide.csv
  classify_rules.py            keyword rules: trade words → description + bucket
  overrides.py                 case-by-case calls (cross-year name/phone evidence, ambiguous names)
  classify.py                  listings_flagged.csv → listings_classified.csv, landuse_by_lot.csv, summary.json
  make_queue.py                adds ids → listings.csv, research_queue.csv
sources/directory_scans/       the LAPL page scans (PDF), one or more per year
outputs/
  sunset-ledger.html           snapshot of the interactive viewer (static; not regenerated by the scripts)
  sunset_business_mix.png      small-multiples chart of business buckets by year
```

Rebuild everything from the repo root:

```
python3 scripts/build.py && python3 scripts/classify.py && python3 scripts/make_queue.py
```

Python 3 standard library only.

## Transcription format (`data/transcriptions/YEAR.txt`)

First line: `# year=YYYY source=...`. Then one listing per line: `address|unit|name as printed|phone`.

- `address` is the printed number; fractions as `1461 1/2`, `1486 1/4`, `1486 3/4`.
- `unit` is blank for building-level listings; otherwise as printed (`Apt 201`, `Rm 4`, `2nd Flr`, or a bare number like `12` in 1964–87).
- Names are kept exactly as printed, including abbreviations (`grocrs`, `restrnt`, `rl est`). Editorial notes are in `[brackets]`.
- Only 1400–2100 inclusive. Sunset Blvd East, Sunset Park and Sunset Place are excluded.

## Key columns in `data/derived/listings.csv`

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| listing_id | `YEAR-ADDRESS-NN`; h/q/t = ½ ¼ ¾ |
| entity_id | links business/institutional listings to `research/research_queue.csv` |
| lot | integer street number; ½ ¼ ¾ addresses roll up to their base lot |
| unit | as printed |
| listing | name as printed |
| caps_business_style | 1987 only: Y = printed in capitals (that directory's business style), N = mixed case (residents) |
| resident_r_flag | trailing “r” in 1956/1960 listings; recorded, not interpreted (abbreviation key not read) |
| use_type | Business, Institutional, Residential, Apartment building, Vacant, Unknown |
| business_desc | what the business is, in plain words |
| business_bucket | one of the 14 buckets below |
| class_confidence | high / medium / low (see HANDOFF.md) |
| class_basis | why the call was made |
| lot_land_use | Commercial, Mixed-use, Residential – multifamily, Residential – single-family, Institutional, Vacant, Unknown |

## Buckets

Food stores · Restaurants & bars · Apparel, shoes & fabric · General & specialty retail · Home furnishings, hardware & appliances · Personal services · Auto sales & service · Health & medical · Finance, insurance & real estate · Professional & business services · Media, printing & entertainment · Light industrial, trades & wholesale · Institutional & civic · Unidentified business. Definitions are in `HANDOFF.md`. They were chosen to map onto NAICS sectors so the data can later be joined to LA Office of Finance business registrations (NAICS-coded).

## Known issues

- **North side at Alvarado, 1906.** 1906 prints Alvarado between 1945 and 2013 on the north side; 1956 has 2035 and 2105 both at “Sunset & Alvarado”. The 1906 listings at 2013/2015 may not be the same lots as later years. Elsewhere, people and cross streets line up by number across 1906–1956.
- **1968 misprints, filed as printed:** EICKHOFF W at 1446 (inside the 1540s run, likely 1546); SUNSET CAMERA at 1643 (1634 every other year, same phone).
- **Single-family vs multifamily** follows the listings: a lot with a ½ address listed that year is multifamily; if the ½ isn't listed the next year, the lot reads single-family.
- **1987:** 18 lots have businesses whose type the directory doesn't reveal (mostly personal names in capitals).
- Image-only pages (read from the scans, no text layer): 1906, 1927, 1956 p.721, 1964 p.41, 1965 p.40, 1968 p.39.
